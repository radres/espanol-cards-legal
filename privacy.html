Spanish Rootcards — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Spanish Rootcards is a Spanish vocabulary flashcard app. It has no accounts and you never sign in.

Your study data

Your study progress and settings are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded.

Anonymous usage analytics

The app sends anonymous, first-party usage analytics so we can see how the app is used and fix what does not work. This covers:

  • that the app was opened, is in use, or went to the background;
  • which screens are opened, for example the home screen or the subscription screen;
  • subscription steps: that the subscription screen was shown, that the subscribe button was tapped, and whether the purchase succeeded, was cancelled, is pending or failed, and whether a restore found an active subscription;
  • the app version, the platform (iOS) and the build type (App Store, TestFlight or development).

Each launch of the app gets a new random session id, which is kept only in memory and never stored on your device, so one session cannot be linked to the next. The analytics contain no account, no name or email, no device identifier and no advertising identifier (IDFA). The app never asks to track you.

Events are sent over HTTPS to yourtechresume.com, a server operated by the same developer as this app. It is not a third-party analytics service. The server records the approximate country of the connection, reported by its network provider, and uses the connection's IP address only to tell test traffic apart from real use. The IP address itself is not stored.

This data is not linked to your identity, is not used for tracking or advertising, and is not sold or shared with anyone. It is deleted automatically after 45 days.

Subscriptions

The Spanish Rootcards Pro subscription is required to use the app and is processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your payment details or your Apple ID. Manage or cancel it in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.
