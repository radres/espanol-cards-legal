Spanish Rootcards — Support

Learn Spanish vocabulary with spaced repetition flashcards. The app requires a Spanish Rootcards Pro subscription; a 30-day free trial is offered on first launch. 1,500+ words across 20 frequency levels, sourced from the RAE CORPES XXI frequency lists.

Get help

Questions, bug reports or feedback: serdaroztetik@gmail.com. Replies usually within a couple of days.

Subscription

Spanish Rootcards Pro is an auto-renewing monthly subscription with a 30-day free trial. It renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel it in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.
